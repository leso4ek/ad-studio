# 24601: a short-drama series inspired by *Les Misérables* (Victor Hugo)

**For your review before any credit is spent.** Pilot: about 43 s, 9:16, made in MAGI-2 (3 clips × 12 s = 90 credits) after casting (≈ 12 credits).

## The look (our own words; no film, studio or character names are used in any prompt)
Hand-painted comic-book animation:
- 3D characters and sets printed like a comic page;
- halftone dot shading in the shadows and slight offset-print colour fringing on edges;
- ink hatching on faces, painted texture on the backdrops;
- motion on twos (a stepped, graphic feel) and motion lines on fast moves;
- the night palette: blue-violet shadows, magenta and cyan neon, amber practical lights.
