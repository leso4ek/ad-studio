## Hook (0–3 s)
- First frame: Aerial of fog rolling over empty grassland; a tiny dark speck in the middle.
- First words: none (no speech until 80 s)
- On-screen text: none
- Hook type: mystery wide + slow tension (a hunter, a deer, a finger on the trigger by 0:12)
- Open loop: Will he shoot? Then: why is he alone?
- First cut: 7.3 s

## Craft
- Cuts per 10 s: 1.85 (ffmpeg scene > 0.32; 47 cuts in 254 s, average shot 5.29 s, first cut 7.3 s)
- Shot types and camera: Static wides, slow push-ins, extreme close-ups of hands and eyes (inserts carry the story)
- Captions: none
- Voice: Sparse; the radio monologue is the climax
- Music / SFX: Ambient; silence used heavily
- Visual style and consistency: One man, one landscape; consistency via the same coat/hat element and weather

## Payoff and end
- Payoff: The radio call reframes everything as post-apocalyptic loneliness (3:34–4:02)
- End: Unanswered call; fade

## Why it works
- Inserts (eye, finger) build tension without dialogue.
- Withholds the premise until the end — the last minute reframes the whole film.
- Silence and stillness make the few sounds land.

## What we would steal
- Hands and eyes in ECU carry a beat better than wide action.
- Hold the premise reveal for the end; open with a concrete, wordless action.
- Use one sound (a radio, a click) as the payoff device.
