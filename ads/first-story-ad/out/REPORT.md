# Sprigly · Sunday standoff

Sprigly · ad-paper-cutout · 20 s · made with Ad Studio (all LLM decisions by Claude)

## The ad
- Premise: Every Sunday Juno and her droopy plant have a silent standoff over the watering can; one capful ends the guessing for both of them.
- Lead hook A: Sunday standoff.
- Turn: Sprigly's measuring cap turns her guess into one clear step (one capful per litre, every two weeks), so feeding stops being a worry.
- Emotion: relief
- Tag line: One capful. No guessing.

Verdict: A clear, honest, finished 20 s story ad; good enough to test, with an obvious next cut.

Test first: hook B. Fixes for a next cut:
- Show the capful: add a watering-can prop to the kit and a 1.5 s shot of the cap filling and pouring in act 3.
- Keep the fern's change modest, the same fern only less droopy, and put the change on Juno: she ticks a 'feed day' note on the fridge.
- Give Juno one specific detail (a name tag on the pot, 'Fernando'; a sticky-note calendar) so she becomes someone.
- Start moving in the first 0.3 s: the fern twitches, or Juno leans in, before the hook card pops.
- 16:9 cut: widen the place with props on the empty sides, and make the end-card product larger.
- Test hook B first ('How much is a bit?'): it names the problem the cap solves; keep A as the control.
- The s4 caption repeats the card word for word; for the sound-off feed, show the caption or the card, not both.
