**Looks like:** thick dark outline plus a wide white die-cut border; compact chunky rounded forms; flat bright fills with a small gloss highlight; very little fine detail, simple cute faces.
**Best for:** short loops and punchy hooks, cute mascots and reaction beats, young, social-first audiences. **Products:** snacks, drinks, stationery, beauty and apps; cheerful, cute, youthful brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Die-cut sticker art: compact, chunky characters and props with soft rounded silhouettes, each wrapped in a thick dark outline and then a wide white border, like a vinyl sticker; flat bright fills with one simple shadow shape and a small white gloss highlight; very little fine detail, no small textures and no thin interior lines; faces are simple and cute, dot eyes and a small mouth; stickers sit on a plain solid or simple patterned background with a soft drop shadow that lifts them off it; a faint laminated sheen.

## Rendering
- Line: thick dark outline, then a wide clean white die-cut border around each character and prop
- Shading: one flat shadow shape per form and a small white gloss highlight
- Colour: bright saturated flat colours on a plain contrasting background; product colours exact
- Texture: smooth with a faint laminated sheen; no fine texture
- Lighting: graphic: a soft drop shadow under each sticker, a gloss highlight on one side

## MOTION block
Bouncy and simple: stickers pop on with a squash and settle, small wiggles and a slight boil on twos, rotations in the picture plane, short loops and held poses; little perspective change, since stickers are flat; the white border travels with its sticker.

## CAMERA habits
Locked flat frontal frame; graphic zoom-punches and in-plane spins; no perspective orbits.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- the white die-cut border disappearing during motion
- thin detailed linework inside forms
- 3D perspective turns of a flat sticker
- printed lettering on the stickers or background
- touching stickers merging into one outline

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character as a set of three die-cut stickers (front, three-quarter, one key pose) on a plain grey sheet, each with its white border; the product as its own sticker with the exact silhouette and colours, label simplified to colour blocks.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
The white border is the signature, and Seedance drops it or merges overlapping stickers into one shape when characters touch. Keep characters apart in the blocking, repeat 'a thick white die-cut border around every character and prop', and keep motion in the picture plane. Sticker sheets attract fake lettering; ban it in NO TEXT.
