# Saturday Morning Cartoon (Traditional 2D animation)

**Looks like:** strong, chunky silhouettes with heroic or comic proportions; bold primary colours and heavy black shadow shapes; dynamic, action-first compositions with low angles; slightly soft broadcast-cel look with painted backgrounds.
**Best for:** energetic hooks, a product as the hero's tool or power-up, kids-and-parents products, nostalgic 80s-90s brands. **Products:** snacks, drinks, toys, sports gear, cleaning products that 'save the day'; loud, fun, high-energy brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
1980s-90s broadcast television cartoon on painted cels: characters have strong chunky silhouettes, thick black outlines and bold primary and secondary colours; cel shading with one hard-edged dark shadow shape and a sharp white highlight on hair, metal and glass; backgrounds are painted with airbrushed skies and gouache detail, more muted than the characters; compositions are dynamic with low angles, diagonal poses and clear foreground action; slightly soft broadcast image with light film grain, warm colour bleed and a faint dust speck now and then.

## Rendering
- Line: thick, confident black outline with slight wobble; inner lines thinner
- Shading: one hard-edged shadow tone per surface, sometimes dark blue-black in action shots; sharp highlight shapes on hair and shiny props
- Colour: bold primaries and secondaries on characters (red, blue, yellow, green, orange); muted painted backgrounds; the product colour exact
- Texture: light film grain, soft broadcast blur, occasional dust specks, airbrush gradients only in backgrounds
- Lighting: a clear hard key with graphic shadow shapes; dramatic coloured sky backgrounds for action beats

## MOTION block
Television animation on twos and threes, economical: strong key poses with fast transitions, held action poses with a background pan behind them, repeated stock-like moves (a run cycle, a turn-and-point), quick speed lines and impact flashes on big beats; mouths in three shapes.

## CAMERA habits
Action grammar: low-angle hero shots, a fast pan following a run, a zoom on a held pose, a dramatic Dutch angle for danger; cuts on action; no handheld shake.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- soft modern 3D lighting on characters
- smooth fully animated motion on ones
- pastel or muted character colours
- photographic depth of field blur
- fake logos and lettering on props and buildings

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet is a model sheet with front, three-quarter, side and an action pose, in the exact outline weight, cel shadows and colours, on plain grey; the product is drawn as a bold hero prop with its exact silhouette and colours, shown in a hand.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance turns action into smooth modern motion and drops the cel shadows into soft gradients; repeat 'cel-shaded, hard shadow shapes, animated on twos and threes'. Fast action can melt hands and the product mid-move, so keep the product held still in a clear pose during its beat. Signs and logos appear unbidden on buildings; keep the set simple.
