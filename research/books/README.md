# Book dramas: classic engines, modern lives

Short-drama series in Ad Studio may be **inspired by a dramatic book** (round 14). Rules:

1. **Public domain only.** The author died more than 70 years ago *and* the book was published before 1930. Name the edition you read and credit the book on episode 1 ("Inspired by *The Count of Monte Cristo* by Alexandre Dumas") and in the description.
2. **Map the engine, not the plot.** Every great drama has one machine underneath: a betrayal, a debt, a secret, a vow. Keep the machine and its reversals. Change the world, jobs, objects and words to today.
3. **Modern = specific.** A job title, an app, an amount, a date, an object you can film (a USB stick, a badge, a group chat). Follow the content-principles skill: a number, name or date beats a summary.
4. **30–50 s per episode** means one location, one turn and one piece of evidence per episode, cut on the most interesting moment.
5. **No real people or companies.** Invent names; a light nod to the book is fine (a ship's name becomes a company's name).
