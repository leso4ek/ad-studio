# What If the Moon Vanished Tonight? (what-if #1)

YouTube, 16:9, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, about 2 min. Stickman toon look built in three.js (original host "Pip"). Narrator: ElevenLabs Brian (free tier).

**The structure:**
- The big question comes in the first 3 s and is answered only at the end.
- Three smaller questions are asked and answered on the way. Each answer is pinned to a red-string board around the big question.
- A twist question bridges to the answer.
- It ends on an open loop.

## Sources (checked 27 Nov 2026)
- **Tides about a third without the Moon:** Inside Science / AIP, "What would happen if there were no moon"; PopSci (a NASA JPL scientist).
- **The day lengthens about 2 ms per century** through tidal braking. This is a standard figure; observed about 1.8 ms per century once other effects are included.
- **Coral mass spawning on the Great Barrier Reef is cued by moonlight,** a few nights after the full moon (widely documented).
- **Obliquity:**
  - With the Moon, about ±1.3° around 23.3°; without it, chaotic from about 0° to 85° (Laskar, Joutel & Robutel 1993, *Nature* 361:615).
  - Later work bounds it more tightly: Lissauer et al. 2012 found the variations are not catastrophic; Li & Batygin 2014 found about 0–45° over billions of years.
  - The script says "newer studies say less, but still tens of degrees".
