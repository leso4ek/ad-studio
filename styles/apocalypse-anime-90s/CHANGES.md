# 90s Apocalypse Anime — decisions

Made from a blank page. Technique base: **cel-anime-80s**. Borrowed moves: **text-flash-cuts** (flash-card editing grammar, drawn in code; no image generation) and **hologram-hud** (rolling numbers, layered UI furniture). The user handed every choice to me ("you decide").

| Section | Base (cel-anime-80s) | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | 80s OVA: neon city, chrome titles, CRT tape | late-90s apocalypse: the ordinary world, text as interruption, interfaces as dread, stillness as horror | the channel tells scary sci-fi stories; the reference's grammar is dread through waiting |
| §2 Materials | 4 layers incl. heavy CRT | cels + flat plates + vector interface layer; mild broadcast tape; inversions; one medium-break per film | 90s broadcast is cleaner; the interface is a new material |
| §3 Colour | saturated neon per time of day | white / teal / black grounds; sunset = last normal day; red only for alarms; one sickly accent pair for the threat | scarce red makes the alarm frightening |
| §4 Type | chrome italic titles; yellow outlined subs | squeezed or stretched heavy serif cards (Noto Serif Display 900 / Shippori Mincho B1 800) in right-angle blocks; Archivo Narrow + Plex Mono UI; white broadcast captions | the reference's typographic signature, with OFL fonts in place of Matisse |
| §5 Motion | 12/8 fps, active | 12 fps, holds of 5–15 s (one ≥ 20 s), snaps and smash cuts | stillness as a tool |
| §6 Camera | speed, pans, city flight | locked holds, extreme low angles up wires, monitor walls, scale reveals | tokusatsu lineage |
| §7 Sound | city pop, slap bass, sax | cicadas/hum beds, sine alarms, silence, classical chorale against horror, calm narrator | the narrator explains a horror case |
| §8 Native moves | backlit cel, speed lines… | Intertitle Barrage, Long Silence, Pattern Alarm, Tribunal Vote, Cross Light, Inner Collapse, Power-Line Horizon | — |
