# Anna Karenina → "Seen"

**Source:** Leo Tolstoy, *Anna Karenina* (1878). Public domain (Tolstoy died in 1910). Credit line: *Inspired by Anna Karenina by Leo Tolstoy.*

## The engine
- **Choice:** a woman in a respectable, cold marriage chooses love in public.
- **The rules:** the man is forgiven and the woman is not. Society punishes her through her child.
- **The weight:** isolation turns love into suspicion, and the book ends at a railway station.

**Handle with care:** the original ending is a suicide. Our adaptation does not show or imply self-harm. Its final turn is a choice to leave and live: she boards a train *away*, phone off. Follow platform rules on sensitive topics.
