# Hamlet → "The Tribute"

**Source:** William Shakespeare, *Hamlet* (c. 1600, First Folio 1623). Public domain. Credit line: *Inspired by Hamlet by William Shakespeare.*

## The engine
- **The ghost:** a dead father names his murderer.
- **The doubt:** is it true, or is grief lying? Hamlet must prove it before he acts.
- **The trap:** a play that re-enacts the murder, to watch the king's face.
- **The spiral:** every delay kills someone else (Polonius, Ophelia).
- **The end:** the truth comes out, and it takes everyone with it.

## Five reversals we keep
1. A natural death is called murder by the dead man himself.
2. The mourning uncle is the killer, and he is now the stepfather.
3. The play meant to catch the killer exposes the hero.
4. The wrong man dies behind a curtain.
5. A duel staged as a reconciliation is a second poisoning.
