# The Count of Monte Cristo → "The Countess"

**Source:** Alexandre Dumas, *Le Comte de Monte-Cristo* (1844–46). Public domain worldwide (Dumas died in 1870). Credit line: *Inspired by The Count of Monte Cristo by Alexandre Dumas.*

## The engine
- **Want:** Edmond wants his ordinary happy life: the promotion to captain and the wedding to Mercédès.
- **Betrayal by three people, each with a small motive:**
  - Danglars: envy over the promotion.
  - Fernand: he loves Mercédès.
  - Villefort: the prosecutor buries the proof because it names his own father.
- **Buried alive:** fourteen years in the Château d'If, cell 34.
- **The mentor:** Abbé Faria teaches him everything and gives him the secret of a treasure.
- **The return:** a stranger with unlimited money takes apart each betrayer with the weapon of their own sin.
- **The cost:** revenge also hurts the innocent. Should he stop?

## Five reversals we keep
1. The best day of his life becomes the day of his arrest.
2. The man who could free him is the one who must bury him.
3. Prison gives him a teacher and a fortune.
4. The stranger everyone courts is the man they destroyed.
5. Each villain is caught by the exact thing they did to him.

## Modern mapping
| Book | Modern |
|---|---|
| Edmond Dantès, sailor about to become captain | **Edie Marsh**, 27, analyst at **Pharaon Capital** (the *Pharaon* was Edmond's ship), promoted the same day she gets engaged |
| Mercédès, his fiancée | **Mateo Reyes**, 28, her fiancé, a paramedic |
| Fernand, rival in love | **Fern Ruiz**, 27, her best friend since school, secretly in love with Mateo |
| Danglars, jealous shipmate | **Dan Glass**, 41, her team lead, who wanted that promotion |
| Villefort, prosecutor protecting his father | **Valerie Fort**, 45, Pharaon's general counsel; the email that clears Edie was sent from her father's account |
| Château d'If, cell 34 | State correctional facility, **cell 34**, 6 years |
| Abbé Faria and the treasure | **Ivo Faria**, 71, a jailed ex-banker; before he dies he writes **24 words** on her palm, the seed phrase of a cold wallet holding **$11 million** |
| The Count of Monte Cristo | **"M. Cristo Holdings"**, the anonymous buyer of Pharaon Capital; "the Countess" to the press |

## Three-episode season (30–50 s each, 9:16)
1. **"Six minutes."** Her promotion toast in the office at 6:02 pm. Fern hands her a USB stick "from Dan". At 6:08 the police arrive: $4.2 million moved under her login. In the glass room Valerie opens the email that would clear her, sees her father's name and deletes it.
   - Cliffhanger: Edie, in cuffs, says "Fern. Tell them." Fern answers "I don't know her."
2. **"Cell 34."** Six years later in a prison cell. Faria, dying, presses a pen into her palm: 24 words. "Eleven million. Nobody knows." He stops breathing. Release day: her phone has a news alert, "Pharaon Capital for sale".
   - Cliffhanger: she types two words to a broker, "Buy it."
3. **"The Countess."** Pharaon's boardroom. Dan, Fern and Valerie wait for the new owner of M. Cristo Holdings. The door opens. Edie puts the old USB stick on the table.
   - Cliffhanger: "You'll recognise this." Then, to Fern: "Hello, Fern."

The open question that carries the season is not "will she get revenge?" but "**what will it cost her?**" Mateo still wears the ring.
