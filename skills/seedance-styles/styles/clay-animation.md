## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Stop-motion claymation: every character and prop is hand-moulded plasticine with visible fingerprints, slight seams and tool marks; sets are miniature practical builds of felt, painted card and wood, lit by small warm practical lamps with soft shadows; shallow depth of field like a macro lens on a tabletop set; colours are saturated but matte, never glossy; the whole film looks physically made and photographed frame by frame.

## Rendering
- Line: no outlines: forms read by modelling and shadow
- Shading: real soft shadows from small lamps, gentle contact shadows under every object
- Colour: matte saturated clay colours, slightly uneven mixing; product colours exact
- Texture: thumbprints, seams, tiny dust and lint, felt fibres, card edges
- Lighting: one warm key practical plus a soft fill; miniature scale shown by shallow focus

## MOTION block
Animated on twos in small stepped jumps with a faint frame-to-frame boil in the clay surface; mouths are replacement shapes; squash and stretch is physical, not elastic.

## CAMERA habits
Locked-off tabletop camera or a slow motorised slider; macro focus pulls; no impossible flying moves.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- smooth CG interpolation
- glossy plastic or rubber surfaces
- perfectly symmetrical faces
- photoreal human skin
- printed text on clay props

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet shows front, three-quarter and side on a grey card backdrop, with the clay colours and the thumbprint texture visible; the product is a clay or real miniature replica with its exact label colours.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance drifts to smooth 3D when the clip is long; repeat 'stepped stop-motion timing, on twos' in MOTION and keep clips 4-8 s.
