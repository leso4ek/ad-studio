## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Chibi super-deformed anime: characters are two to two-and-a-half heads tall, with a huge round head, big simple eyes with one large highlight, a tiny mouth, a small soft body, stubby arms with mitten hands and tiny feet; detail is pared back to the essentials of hair shape and outfit colour; clean thick rounded line; flat bright colour with one soft shadow tone and pink blush ovals on the cheeks; simple, uncluttered backgrounds painted softly or reduced to flat colour; props stay at their real size, so they look comically big in the small hands.

## Rendering
- Line: clean, thick, rounded line of even weight; few inner lines
- Shading: one soft shadow tone, blush ovals, a single big highlight in each eye
- Colour: bright, clean, slightly pastel-shifted palette; outfit and hair colours exact and few; product colours exact
- Texture: none on characters; soft brush texture in simple backgrounds
- Lighting: even, cheerful daylight; reactions can switch to a flat colour background (dark blue for gloom, pink for joy)

## MOTION block
Snappy, bouncy motion on twos: quick hops, a wobble of the whole body, a big head tilt; reactions are sudden pose changes to an exaggerated face held for a beat (sweat drop, puffed cheeks, sparkly eyes, crying streams), with small pop effects; walks are tiny fast steps.

## CAMERA habits
Mostly frontal medium-full shots that keep the whole little body in frame; quick punch-in zooms on reaction faces; simple side-on staging; no dramatic perspective.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- normal adult body proportions creeping back
- realistic hands with separated fingers
- detailed shading and rendered hair strands
- glossy 3D vinyl-figure surfaces
- speech bubbles and comic lettering on reactions

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet shows the chibi figure front, three-quarter, side and back with the head-to-body ratio marked, plus four reaction faces; the product is drawn at its real size beside the chibi so scale stays consistent; places are simplified backgrounds.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance drifts back toward normal anime proportions in longer clips and when the character walks; state '2.5 heads tall' in STYLE and CAST RULE. It also turns chibi figures into glossy 3D collectible toys; repeat 'flat 2D, no 3D'. Symbol effects (sweat drops, sparkles) may arrive as text or emoji; describe them as drawn shapes.
