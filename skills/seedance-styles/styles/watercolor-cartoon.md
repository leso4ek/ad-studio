**Looks like:** transparent washes that pool darker at their edges; feathered wet-into-wet blooms and soft bleeds; cold-press paper grain showing through every colour; loose brush or pen lines that do not close every shape.
**Best for:** gentle emotional stories, home, garden and plant products, morning and outdoor scenes, calm brands that want warmth without polish. **Products:** natural, botanical, tea, skincare and baby products; soft, honest, slow-living brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Hand-painted watercolour cartoon on cold-press paper: transparent washes laid in two or three glazes, each drying with a darker hard edge where the pigment pooled; soft wet-into-wet blooms in skies and backgrounds; the white of the paper left bare for highlights instead of painted white; a loose sepia brush line around characters that breaks and skips; visible paper tooth and a faint granulation in the blues and earth colours; backgrounds simpler and paler than the figures; the picture looks painted on one sheet and photographed flat under even daylight.

## Rendering
- Line: thin, loose sepia or dark-grey brush line, broken in places, thicker on the shadow side; backgrounds mostly without line
- Shading: a second darker glaze of the same hue for shadows, with hard dried edges; no airbrushed falloff
- Colour: limited palette of six to eight pigments, mixed on the paper; warm light, cool violet-blue shadows; product colours laid flat and exact
- Texture: cold-press paper tooth, granulation, cauliflower blooms, a few drips and dry-brush streaks on edges
- Lighting: implied by leaving white paper; one soft warm key from a window; shadows painted, never rendered

## MOTION block
Animated on twos with held drawings between gestures; the paper texture stays fixed to the frame like a sheet under the camera while washes inside characters shimmer very slightly from drawing to drawing; backgrounds are a single painted plate that never repaints itself.

## CAMERA habits
Locked or a slow lateral pan across a painted background plate; gentle push-ins on faces; layered parallax at most two planes deep; no handheld shake, no orbiting.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- smooth digital gradients in the sky
- glossy specular highlights on skin or glass
- crisp vector outlines
- paper texture that crawls or swims across the frame
- opaque white paint for highlights

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet shows front, three-quarter and side painted on one sheet of cold-press paper with the same pigment set and the same line colour; the product sheet paints the bottle with its exact cap and glass colours and keeps its label a flat area of colour.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance smooths washes into airbrushed gradients and renders depth with 3D shading as the clip goes on, and paper grain tends to slide with the camera; repeat 'hard dried wash edges, fixed paper grain, on twos' in MOTION and keep camera moves small. Faces lose their loose line first, so keep a face reference in every shot.
