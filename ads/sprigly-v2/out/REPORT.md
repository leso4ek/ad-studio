# Sprigly · Sunday standoff (directed)

Sprigly · ad-paper-cutout · 20 s · made with Ad Studio (all LLM decisions by Claude)

## The ad
- Premise: Every Sunday Juno and her droopy plant have a silent standoff over the watering can; one capful ends the guessing for both of them.
- Lead hook A: Sunday standoff.
- Turn: Sprigly's measuring cap turns her guess into one clear step (one capful per litre, every two weeks), so feeding stops being a worry.
- Emotion: relief
- Tag line: One capful. No guessing.

Verdict: A directed cut: varied sizes, the pour shown and the name married to the rule fix v1's two biggest gaps; the character is still generic.

Test first: hook B. Fixes for a next cut:
- Re-run the story stage to give Juno one specific detail (a named pot, a 'feed day' sticky note) and carry it into two shots.
- Frame s9 lower or move the 'spring and summer' subline so it clears her bun.
- Add a smaller 'notice' pose to the kit (eyes and a slight lean) for turn beats; arms-up is too big for a calm product.
- Keep the payoff fern only slightly perkier than the droopy one (a modest variant), not fully lush.
- Make the capful insert's stream thicker so the pour reads at phone size.

## Claims and their basis
- "One capful per litre" (basis: Dose is one capful (5 ml) per litre of water)
- "Every two weeks in spring and summer" (basis: Label directions: feed every two weeks in spring and summer, monthly in autumn and winter)
- "With seaweed extract" (basis: Contains seaweed extract plus NPK 3-1-2)
