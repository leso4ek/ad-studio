# Where does the water go? (3D explainer)

Sprigly · ad-voxel-3d · 18.5 s · made with Ad Studio (all LLM decisions by Claude)

## The ad
- Premise: Where does the water go when you water your fern? A cutaway pot follows it from the spout to the saucer.
- Lead hook A: Where does it go?
- Turn: The cutaway shows the feed can only reach the roots in the water, which is why Sprigly is dosed per litre.
- Emotion: curiosity
- Tag line: One capful per litre.

## Scorecard (26/35)
| Rule | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook in 1-3 s | 4 | A plain question over a half-cut pot; the camera is moving from frame one. |
| 2 All five acts | 4 | Question, soil, roots, drainage, tag: a chain rather than a drama, as the format intends. |
| 3 A character we identify with | 2 | No character: the viewer is the character. An explainer trades this rule for curiosity. |
| 4 The product causes the turn | 3 | The cutaway makes the per-litre dose make sense, but the product only appears on the end card. |
| 5 Plant / pulse / reveal / tag | 3 | Reveal and tag only, by design: an explainer that sells in the middle stops being trusted. |
| 6 One emotion | 5 | Curiosity, answered calmly. |
| 7 Kind contrast and honest claims | 5 | Plain horticulture, no promise about results; one label claim. |

Verdict: A clean explainer that does its job and sells softly; score it on watch time and saves rather than on the story-ad rules.

Test first: hook A. Fixes for a next cut:
- Lengthen to 45-75 s with three more stops (overwatering, a pot with no hole, the feed dose) to match the format's real length.
- Put the bottle on the ledge from the first frame as a quiet plant.
- Add one recurring small prop (a fallen leaf riding the water) for continuity.
