# Les Misérables → "24601"

**Source:** Victor Hugo, *Les Misérables* (1862). Public domain (Hugo died in 1885). Credit line: *Inspired by Les Misérables by Victor Hugo.*

## The engine
- **A man made into a number:** Jean Valjean, prisoner 24601, did 19 years for a loaf of bread.
- **Grace that he did not earn:** the Bishop of Digne feeds him. Valjean steals his silver, and the bishop tells the police it was a gift: "You forgot the candlesticks." He buys his soul for good.
- **The law that cannot forgive:** Inspector Javert believes a man never changes and hunts him for 17 years.
- **A promise to the dying:** he swears to Fantine that he will save her daughter Cosette.
- **The barricade and the sewers:** he carries the wounded Marius through the sewers of Paris.
- **The solution:** Javert, saved by the man he hunts, cannot reconcile mercy with law and lets him go. Mercy is stronger than the law, and it costs the one who cannot bend.
