# Vector Cartoon (Digital and vector)
<!-- generated from studio/styles/catalog/vector-cartoon.json by studio/bin/catalog.mjs; edit the JSON -->

**Looks like:** smooth geometric curves with sharp tidy corners; flat fills with one hard shadow shape; clean uniform outlines or none; layered flat sets with parallax.
**Best for:** fast, clear explainer stories, apps and services, bright comedy with simple acting. **Products:** apps, digital services, household and food products; clear, upbeat, modern brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Vector cartoon: characters and sets built from clean geometric curves with crisp outlines of one uniform dark weight, sharp corners and tidy joins; flat solid fills from a controlled palette of eight to twelve colours, with one hard-edged shadow shape and one highlight shape per form; no texture, no grain, no gradients apart from one soft background gradient; rounded bendy limbs, big expressive eyes and simplified hands; backgrounds are layered flat shapes that slide with parallax; every shape looks drawn with clean curves, perfectly smooth.

## Rendering
- Line: uniform-weight dark outlines (or coloured lines a shade darker than the fill), perfectly smooth curves
- Shading: one hard-edged flat shadow shape and one highlight shape per form
- Colour: 8-12 flat colours with clear value separation between figure and ground; product colours exact
- Texture: none; perfectly clean flat colour
- Lighting: graphic: a consistent light side expressed by the shadow shapes, no light falloff

## MOTION block
Rigged cut-out or tweened animation: smooth body moves on ones with clean ease curves, mouth and hand swaps on twos; bendy limbs that curve without elbows; secondary motion on hair and leaves; shapes keep their crisp edges and outline weight while moving.

## CAMERA habits
Flat 2D camera: pans, trucks and zooms on the picture plane, with parallax between background layers; no 3D rotation of the set.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- soft blurred edges and motion blur
- painterly texture or grain
- 3D volumetric lighting
- outline weight changing between frames
- random interface icons and text on screens

## Element sheets for consistency
Characters as a flat turnaround (front, three-quarter, side) with the palette swatches and a mouth chart; places as one layered flat background with foreground, middle and back layers; the product as a flat vector icon with its exact silhouette and colours, the label simplified.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Usually holds well, but Seedance adds depth: soft shading, motion blur and a 3D camera turn. Write 'flat 2D plane, no 3D camera rotation, no motion blur' in CAMERA and MOTION. Outlines wobble on fast action; keep moves moderate and clips 4-8 s.
