**Looks like:** loose hand-drawn marker and ballpoint lines; simple shapes: bean bodies, dot eyes, stick-like limbs; playful marks: motion lines, sparkles, little hearts, sweat drops; spontaneous details scribbled around the subject.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Hand-drawn doodle animation on plain white sketchbook paper: everything is drawn with a black felt-tip marker line of slightly uneven weight, with shapes that do not quite close and corners that overshoot; characters are simple, with bean-shaped bodies, dot eyes and a curved line for a mouth; colour is added by quick marker fills that miss the lines and leave white gaps and streaks; playful marks float around the action (motion lines, sparkles, small hearts, sweat drops, swirls); backgrounds are a few sketchy lines, not a full scene; lots of white paper.

## Rendering
- Line: black felt-tip marker, slightly uneven weight, overshooting corners, gaps where shapes do not close
- Shading: none, or a few scribbled hatch lines under objects as a shadow
- Colour: three or four marker colours scribbled in with streaks and gaps outside the lines; the product filled in its exact green and yellow
- Texture: white sketchbook paper, marker streaks, slight bleed where colours overlap
- Lighting: none: flat paper; emphasis comes from sparkle marks and motion lines instead of light

## MOTION block
Animated on threes with a strong line boil: every line is redrawn each frame and wiggles even on holds; actions are snappy, with a few drawings per move and big held poses; marks pop on in one or two frames; colour fills jitter slightly inside their lines.

## CAMERA habits
Flat and front-on like a page; quick zooms into a detail, a slide to the next doodle; transitions where a new drawing scribbles itself over the old one; no perspective camera moves.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- clean vector lines and perfect circles
- smooth tweened motion with no line boil
- 3D volume or soft shading
- handwritten words, labels and speech bubbles
- a busy fully drawn background

## Element sheets for consistency
A doodle sheet of the woman (front, three-quarter, side and three expressions) with her bun, orange top and bean-body proportions fixed and a colour swatch for each marker; the fern and the product drawn in the same marker style, the product in its exact colours.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance smooths the line boil away after a second or two (the drawing turns into a clean vector puppet) and loves to add handwritten words and speech bubbles; repeat 'redrawn every frame, wiggling line boil, on threes' and 'no letters or speech bubbles'. Simple dot-eye faces vary from shot to shot, so keep one face reference per shot.
