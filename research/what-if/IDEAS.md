# What-if ideas (stickman three.js channel)

**The format:**
- Ask the big question in the first 3 s, then deliberately don't answer it.
- Answer 3–4 smaller questions. Each answer is a piece of the big one, pinned to a board with red string.
- The big question is answered only in the last 15 s.
- End on the next question (an open loop).

| # | Big question (answered last) | Small questions in between (answered on the way) | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What if the Moon vanished tonight?** | Would the oceans flood? · Would nights go black? · Would our days get shorter? · Then why do scientists call the Moon a stabiliser? | Everyone expects a disaster. The twist is "tonight, almost nothing, but over deep time, the seasons break". |
| 2 | What if you jumped into a hole through the Earth? | How hot is the middle? · Would you fall forever? · How long is the trip (about 38–42 min)? | Ends on *where you'd come out*: mostly ocean. |
| 3 | What if humans could hibernate like bears? | How do bears keep their muscles? · How slow can a heart go? · How much food would we skip? | Ends on: could astronauts sleep their way to Mars? |
| 4 | What if a coin fell from the top of a skyscraper onto your head? | How fast can a coin fall? · Why does it tumble? · What does air do to it? | A fast myth-buster; ends on "it stings, it doesn't kill". |
| 5 | What if all the ice on Earth melted at once? | How high would the sea rise? · Which coasts vanish? · Would it get hotter? | Ends on the new map of the world. |

**Picked:** idea 1, the Moon. It has the biggest stakes, the most visual answers (tides, dark nights, a spinning top) and an honest twist.
