# Retro-Futuristic (Retro and nostalgic)

**Looks like:** streamlined machines with fins, domes and chrome trim; geometric atomic-age forms: boomerangs, starbursts, ovals; pastel and turquoise palettes with warm sunset skies; an optimistic mid-century picture of tomorrow.
**Best for:** 'the future of your kitchen' style reveals, tech, gadget and appliance products, playful sci-fi premises, bold, upbeat brand stories. **Products:** appliances, gadgets, drinks, travel and smart-home products; optimistic, inventive, upbeat brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Mid-century retro-futurist illustration in motion: a tomorrow imagined around 1958, with streamlined machines that have tail fins, bubble domes, chrome trim and rounded rocket shapes; homes with boomerang tables, starburst clocks and glass walls opening onto upswept towers; characters drawn with clean confident lines and stylised long proportions; flat gouache colour in turquoise, coral, mint, cream and atomic orange, with airbrushed glows only on chrome and sky; soft printed grain and slight colour fade, like a painted magazine spread of the world to come.

## Rendering
- Line: clean, confident ink line with tapered ends; machines drawn with ruler-straight edges and perfect ellipses
- Shading: flat colour with one painted shadow tone; chrome as hard bands of white and sky colour; soft airbrush only on glows
- Colour: turquoise, coral, mint, cream and atomic orange under a warm peach-to-teal sky; the product's green and yellow kept exact and treated as a sleek device colour
- Texture: light print grain and paper warmth, faint colour fade; chrome stays crisp
- Lighting: bright optimistic daylight or a sunset glow; neon-like halos on domes and dials

## MOTION block
Animated on twos with smooth, gliding machine moves and springy character gestures; devices move in clean arcs and slide on rails, dials sweep, lights blink in sequence; nothing is grimy or shaky.

## CAMERA habits
Wide showroom framings with low horizon lines; slow sweeping crane-like pans across the world of tomorrow; graceful push-ins on the gadget; symmetrical compositions for reveals.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- dark grimy dystopian tech
- modern glass smartphones and flat-screen interfaces
- glowing holographic UI text
- photoreal 3D chrome reflections
- slogans or lettering on billboards and machines

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character in front, three-quarter and side with the stylised long proportions fixed; the place as a wide of the home of tomorrow and a detail of its signature gadget; the product redrawn sleek but with its exact bottle shape and colours, plus one view in the retro machine context.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance pulls the future toward modern sci-fi (dark blue, holograms, glass phones) and renders chrome as photoreal 3D; repeat 'imagined in 1958, flat gouache, painted chrome bands' and name the props (fins, domes, starburst clocks). Billboards and control panels fill with fake lettering, so say 'no words on machines or signs'.
