## Craft
- Cuts per 10 s: detector 8 cuts / 77.5 s = **1.03**; most changes are continuous zooms and scale jumps, not hard cuts.
- Shot types and camera: first-person POV (own hands in frame), macro zoom-ins through scales (mm → nm → fm), pull-backs to an aerial city view.
- Captions: no speech captions. Two text layers: (1) **HUD** top-left — small caps label + big serif counter (3 decimals) + small caps "fact line"; (2) **italic serif sentence** centred low ("Normal chemistry can no longer continue.").
- Voice: none; 0 words/s.
- Music: ambient/tense bed; the colour flip at 7 s is the "drop".
- Visual style: soft stylised 3D (rounded low-poly people, painterly trees) for the real world; glossy pink molecular renders for the "inside". The HUD is the constant that ties both together.
- Transitions: scale zooms and colour inversion; no wipes.

- Payoff: at 70 s the counter reaches 00.000 and everything is back: "CENTRAL PARK · 5 SECONDS LATER".

## Why it works
- A **visible countdown** is a promise of a payoff at a known time; nobody leaves at 00.300.
- **Scale ladder**: every jump (mm → nm → fm) is a new "and smaller?" question, about one every 1–3 s through 23–30 s.
- **POV hands** put the viewer inside the disaster; the "what if" is about *you*.

## What we would steal

- End on the first frame's composition with a "N SECONDS LATER" label so it loops.
