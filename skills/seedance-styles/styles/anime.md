**Looks like:** clean, thin, even linework with sharp hair clumps; large eyes with layered highlights and small noses and mouths; two-tone cel shading with a crisp shadow edge; detailed painted backgrounds with soft light and bloom.
**Best for:** slice-of-life moments, quiet emotional turns, young urban audiences, products in a daily routine. **Products:** drinks, skincare, stationery, plants, tech accessories, food; warm, sincere or gently funny brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Modern Japanese TV anime: clean thin digital lines of even weight, characters with large eyes holding two or three highlight dots, small noses and mouths, hair in sharp separated clumps with a single shine band; two-tone cel shading with a crisp shadow edge and a soft blush on cheeks; backgrounds are detailed hand-painted environments with natural light, slightly richer in texture than the characters; balanced, clear colour with gentle soft bloom on bright windows and sunlight; it looks like a 2D production composited over painted backgrounds, never 3D.

## Rendering
- Line: thin, even, clean digital line; coloured lines (brown, dark red) on skin and hair edges in bright light
- Shading: two-tone cel shading with a sharp shadow edge, one highlight shape on hair, small specular dots in eyes
- Colour: balanced, natural palette, a touch brighter than life; skin warm and flat; product colours exact
- Texture: characters clean; backgrounds painted with soft brush texture and fine detail
- Lighting: natural light from windows or sun with soft bloom and light shafts in the compositing; warm afternoon or cool morning keys

## MOTION block
Pose-to-pose on twos and threes with eased in-betweens; held shots where only the hair, eyes or a hand moves; blinks and small breaths; quick character turns with hair following a frame later; wind moving hair and leaves gently; no rubbery squash and stretch.

## CAMERA habits
Anime grammar: still compositions with slow pans over painted backgrounds, gentle push-ins, cut-ins to eyes and hands, low-angle sky shots, a slight focus rack between foreground and character; mostly locked, never handheld.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- smooth 3D-rendered faces and plastic skin
- realistic nose and lip modelling
- Western cartoon rubber limbs
- Japanese-looking fake text on signs and packaging
- eye shape and hair colour changing between shots

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet is a settei-style turnaround (front, three-quarter, side, back) in clean line and flat cel colours on white, with a colour chip row and two expressions; places are painted background plates (wide and reverse angle); the product is drawn in anime prop style with its exact proportions, cap and colours.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance fills signs, labels and posters with fake Japanese-looking text; keep walls and packaging plain and say so in NO TEXT. Faces drift between shots (eye size, hair parting), so the turnaround goes in every shot. Long clips drift toward glossy 3D-anime rendering; repeat 'flat 2D cel shading, never 3D'.
