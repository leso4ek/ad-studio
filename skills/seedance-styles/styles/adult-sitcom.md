## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Prime-time animated sitcom look: simplified characters with uniform medium-weight black outlines, rounded heads, large white eyes with small dot pupils, overbite or simple line mouths, four-finger hands; flat cel colour with no shading except a rare single shadow tone; everyday suburban settings drawn with clean straight lines and slightly simplified perspective, a believable lived-in clutter of plain objects; bright, even, slightly saturated television colours; backgrounds outlined in a thinner line than the characters; it looks like a clean digital-ink television production.

## Rendering
- Line: uniform medium black line on characters, thinner lighter line on backgrounds; no pressure taper
- Shading: flat, no shading; at most one shadow tone under chins and furniture for night or drama
- Colour: bright, even TV colours; skin tones in simple flat hues; each character in one fixed outfit palette; product colours exact
- Texture: none: clean digital fills, no grain
- Lighting: even, high-key interior light; time of day shown by window colour only

## MOTION block
Limited TV animation on twos: characters mostly stand and talk with blinks, head tilts and small hand gestures, mouths cycling through a few shapes; actions are quick pose changes; comedic beats land on a held deadpan stare of one to two seconds.

## CAMERA habits
Sitcom coverage: eye-level medium two-shots, over-the-shoulder singles, a wide establishing shot of the room; straight cuts, rare slow push-ins for a punchline; no handheld, no dramatic angles.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- shaded 3D-looking faces
- realistic eyes with irises and lashes
- dramatic low angles and lens flares
- anime-style hair highlights
- readable posters, cereal boxes or signs generated in the background

## Element sheets for consistency
Each character sheet shows front, three-quarter and side in the exact outline weight and flat colours, plus a mouth chart of five shapes and two expressions (deadpan, alarmed); places are wide and reverse views of the room with the clutter fixed; the product is drawn flat with its exact silhouette and colours.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Background clutter fills with fake labels and readable gibberish; ask for plain unlabeled objects. Seedance animates these characters too smoothly and gives them too much acting; specify small gestures and held stares. Small-pupil eyes can grow irises between shots, so send the sheet with every shot.
