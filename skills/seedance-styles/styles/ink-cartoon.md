# Ink Cartoon (Comic and graphic art)

**Looks like:** lively pressure-varied pen strokes that overshoot corners; cross-hatching and stippling for tone; loose wash colour under the line; rough, tactile paper with blots and construction lines.
**Best for:** wry, observational stories, craft, food and heritage brands, quiet, characterful narration. **Products:** artisanal and natural products (coffee, tea, bakery, garden, stationery); thoughtful, witty brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Hand-inked cartoon on paper: a lively pen-and-ink line with varying pressure, wiry and slightly scratchy strokes that overshoot at corners; tone built from cross-hatching and stippling, densest in the shadows; colour, where used, is a loose watercolour or ink wash laid under the lines, filling them unevenly and leaving white paper showing; warm off-white paper with visible grain, the odd ink blot and faint pencil construction lines; backgrounds drawn in the same pen, with fewer lines toward the edges of the frame; rough, tactile and handmade.

## Rendering
- Line: dip-pen line with pressure variation, overshoots and broken strokes, darker at overlaps
- Shading: cross-hatching and stippling; hatching direction follows the form and stays consistent
- Colour: loose transparent washes in a few warm and earthy tones, white paper showing; product colours exact but washed
- Texture: off-white cold-press paper grain, ink blots, pencil construction lines
- Lighting: implied by hatching density: one side lit, the other hatched

## MOTION block
Drawn animation on twos with a gentle, controlled line boil, each drawing redrawn so strokes shimmer slightly; holds keep the boil; hatching keeps its direction from frame to frame; wash colour stays put while the line moves.

## CAMERA habits
Mostly locked, or slow pans across a drawn background; an occasional slow push-in; the paper texture stays the same sheet.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- clean vector lines
- hatching swimming in random directions
- flat digital colour fills
- smooth 3D shading under the ink
- handwritten gibberish in margins and on signs

## Element sheets for consistency
Characters as a pen model sheet (front, three-quarter, side) with light hatching kept off the face so the features stay readable, plus a washed colour version; places as one inked wide with the hatching pattern; the product drawn in pen with its exact silhouette and label colours in wash.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Seedance either freezes the line into clean vector work or lets the hatching melt into noise. Ask for 'a gentle line boil on twos' explicitly, keep hatching sparse on faces so they stay recognisable, and keep clips 4-6 s. Fake handwriting appears in margins and on signs; ban it in NO TEXT.
