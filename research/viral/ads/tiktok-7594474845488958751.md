## Why it works
- A complete sitcom mini-episode (goal → help → song → twist → twist) where the product is the help the father gives.

- The double twist after the product tag-in keeps people watching through the ad's middle and gives the comments something to discuss.

## What we would steal
- Give the product to the hero as a mentor's gift in the first 15 s, then let a set-piece dramatise what happens *without* it.
- Switch format once (sitcom → musical / stage) at ~20 % to reset attention; switch back for the ending.
- Put a story twist after the product's big moment so the ad doesn't end on the sell.
