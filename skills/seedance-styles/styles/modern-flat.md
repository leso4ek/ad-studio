# Modern Flat (Digital and vector)
<!-- generated from studio/styles/catalog/modern-flat.json by studio/bin/catalog.mjs; edit the JSON -->

**Looks like:** pared-back figures with small heads and long simple limbs; bold colour blocks, almost no shading; minimal or blank faces; generous negative space and editorial layout.
**Best for:** calm explainers and product benefits, fintech, health-adjacent and wellness services, ads that rely on voice-over and layout more than acting. **Products:** services, apps and clean consumer goods; calm, trustworthy, contemporary brands.

## STYLE block (paste verbatim into every shot prompt and every sheet prompt)
Modern flat illustration in motion: pared-back figures with small heads, long simple limbs and no outlines, faces reduced to two dot eyes or left blank; bold blocks of colour from a restrained contemporary palette, one warm accent against soft neutrals and two mid-tones; almost no shading, only an occasional flat darker shape for a shadow or a fold; a subtle fine grain inside the fills; generous negative space; objects simplified to geometric silhouettes; clean, editorial compositions, like an illustrated explainer.

## Rendering
- Line: no outlines: shapes meet edge to edge
- Shading: almost none; a single flat darker shape for a fold or cast shadow
- Colour: restrained palette: soft neutrals, two mid-tones, one warm accent; product colours exact
- Texture: fine subtle grain inside the fills, otherwise clean
- Lighting: no lighting model: flat colour and contrast carry the hierarchy

## MOTION block
Smooth motion-graphics timing: eased transitions, shapes that morph gracefully, characters in simple gestures and loops; limited body acting, emotion carried by posture and colour; overlapping action on hair and plants; nothing jitters.

## CAMERA habits
Flat side or frontal views, slow horizontal pans, scale reveals that pull out to show more of the layout; shape-wipe transitions are added in the edit.

## Avoid (name these instances only when the shot is at risk)
- detailed eyes and mouths appearing mid-shot
- gradient-heavy 3D shading
- outlines appearing around shapes
- cluttered busy backgrounds
- fake icons and lettering on screens and posters

## Element sheets for consistency
Characters as a flat side, front and three-quarter set with the face formula written in the prompt (two dot eyes, no mouth) and the palette listed; places as one wide editorial composition with plenty of negative space; the product as a simple geometric silhouette in its exact colours.

## Where Seedance goes wrong in this style
Minimal faces are the point, but Seedance fills them in with eyes and mouths after a few seconds and adds gradients. Write 'faces stay minimal: two dot eyes, no mouth' in STYLE and the CAST RULE. Acting is limited, so put the emotional turn in posture, framing and the colour accent rather than expressions.
