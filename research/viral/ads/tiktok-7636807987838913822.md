## Why it works
- "But then…" chain: every 4–6 s a worse disaster; each one is a new open loop.
- It parodies a format everyone knows (the infomercial problem/solution), so the product reveal is expected and still funny.
- The benefit ("we do the dangerous part") is told entirely by showing the absurd alternative.

## What we would steal
- Second-person "Ever try to [X] on your own? But then…" chain with an escalation every ≤ 6 s, ending in "There's got to be a better way."
- Tiny deadpan "DO NOT…" supers as joke disclaimers.
- If the brand opens the ad, hold it back afterwards and return only for the reveal + 2-line slogan.
